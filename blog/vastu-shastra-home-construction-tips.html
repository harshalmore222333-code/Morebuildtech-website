Vastu Shastra Basics for Home Construction

Before we dive into the tips, it's essential to understand the basics of vastu shastra. Vastu shastra is based on the concept of five elements: earth, water, fire, air, and space. A harmonious balance of these elements is necessary to create a positive and peaceful living environment.

Understanding the Five Elements

  • Earth element: represents stability and grounding
  • Water element: represents flow and movement
  • Fire element: represents energy and transformation
  • Air element: represents intellectual and communication
  • Space element: represents spiritual and emotional growth

Vastu Shastra Tips for Plot Selection

When selecting a plot for your new home construction, it's crucial to consider the vastu shastra principles. A plot with a square or rectangular shape is considered ideal, as it represents balance and stability. Avoid plots with irregular shapes, as they can lead to instability and chaos.

Vastu Shastra Tips for Home Design

A well-designed home can bring harmony and balance to your life. Consider the following vastu shastra tips for home design: ensure that the main door is located in the north or east direction, as it brings positive energy into the home. The living room should be located in the north or east direction, while the bedroom should be located in the south or west direction.

Color Scheme and Lighting

The color scheme and lighting of your home can also impact the vastu shastra of your space. Use calming colors such as blue, green, or neutral tones to create a peaceful atmosphere. Avoid using bright or bold colors, as they can create tension and anxiety.

Vastu Shastra Tips for Interior Design

When it comes to interior design, vastu shastra principles can help create a harmonious and balanced space. Consider the following tips: use mirrors wisely, as they can create the illusion of a larger space. Avoid placing mirrors opposite the main door, as it can reflect negative energy away from the home.

Vastu Shastra Tips for Construction Materials

The construction materials used in your new home can also impact the vastu shastra of your space. Consider using natural materials such as wood, stone, or brick, as they bring a sense of warmth and coziness to the space. Avoid using synthetic materials, as they can create a cold and uninviting atmosphere.

Vastu Shastra Tips for Budgeting and Costing

When it comes to new home construction, budgeting and costing are crucial aspects to consider. The cost of constructing a new home in Mumbai can range from INR 2,500 to INR 5,000 per square foot, depending on the materials and design used. Consider consulting with a vastu expert or interior designer to ensure that your new home is designed and constructed in accordance with vastu shastra principles.

Vastu Shastra Tips for Maintenance and Upkeep

Maintenance and upkeep are essential to ensure that your new home remains harmonious and balanced. Consider the following vastu shastra tips: regularly clean and declutter your space, as clutter can create negative energy. Use plants and flowers to purify the air and create a peaceful atmosphere.
