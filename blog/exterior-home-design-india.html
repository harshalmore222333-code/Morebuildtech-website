Exterior Home Design India: Tips & Ideas

August 11, 202610 min readMoreBuildtech Expert TeamMumbai

When it comes to exterior home design India, there are countless options to choose from. As a homeowner in Mumbai or any other part of Maharashtra, you want your home's exterior to reflect your personal style and complement the surrounding architecture. In this blog post, we'll explore the latest trends and ideas in exterior home design, including paint colors and elevation ideas that will give your home a stunning makeover.

Exterior Home Design Trends in India

In recent years, Indian homeowners have been opting for more modern and sleek exterior designs. This includes the use of minimalistic facades, large windows, and bold paint colors. If you're looking to give your home a contemporary look, consider using a combination of materials such as glass, steel, and concrete.

Choose the Right Paint Colors

Popular Paint Color Options

When it comes to choosing the right paint colors for your home's exterior, there are several factors to consider. You'll want to think about the color of your roof, the style of your home, and the surrounding environment. Some popular paint color options for exterior home design in India include shades of white, cream, and beige. These colors are not only aesthetically pleasing but also help to reflect the sun's rays and keep your home cool.

On average, the cost of painting a home's exterior in Mumbai can range from ₹50,000 to ₹2,00,000, depending on the size of the house and the type of paint used.

Home Elevation Ideas for a Stunning Exterior

A well-designed elevation can make a significant difference in your home's exterior appearance. Some popular home elevation ideas in India include the use of arches, columns, and ornate details. If you're looking for a more modern look, consider using clean lines, minimalistic details, and large windows.

Exterior Design Ideas for Small Homes

Just because you have a small home doesn't mean you can't have a stunning exterior design. Some exterior design ideas for small homes in India include the use of vertical gardens, compact balconies, and sleek facades. You can also use light-colored paint colors and minimalistic details to create the illusion of a larger space.

Exterior Lighting Ideas to Enhance Your Home's Ambiance

Exterior lighting can play a significant role in enhancing your home's ambiance and security. Some popular exterior lighting ideas in India include the use of solar-powered lights, string lights, and LED lights. You can place these lights around your home's perimeter, in your garden, or along your walkway.

Maintenance and Upkeep of Your Home's Exterior

Maintenance and upkeep are essential to ensuring your home's exterior remains in good condition. This includes regular cleaning, painting, and repairs. You should also inspect your home's exterior regularly to identify any potential issues before they become major problems.
